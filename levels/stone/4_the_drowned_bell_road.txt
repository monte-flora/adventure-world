theme: crystal
name: The Drowned Bell Road
stone: tide
land: drowned_mere
needs: dash, climb, pound
about: Teach: a sliding stone over a shallow dry pit. Test: a long ride over spikes, then a lift up to a shelf. Twist: two rides over a lava pool with an island between, then the ladder up. Finale: ride over lava, lift up the ledge, dig out the Tide Stone, ride the spike pit, pull the lever and drop through the gate home. Secret: a gem arrow points to a cracked floor stone and a chest room below.
