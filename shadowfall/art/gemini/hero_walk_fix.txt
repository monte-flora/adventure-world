FIXING ONE FRAME OF A HERO PICTURE (Gemini's picture editing)
Use this in the same Gemini chat, right after any hero_* picture (walk, idle, attack...), when one frame is off.
Copy the lines between the dashes, change the [brackets], and send.
-----
Edit this picture. Change only frame [2] (counting from the left); keep the other frames exactly as they are, pixel for pixel.
In frame [2]: [the lantern is in the wrong hand: put it in his right hand at hip height, like frames 1, 3 and 4].
Keep the same character, colours, size and ground line as the other frames, the same pixel-art style, and the same flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background. Keep the same grid (4x1, or 3x1 for an attack or lantern raise) with a clear gap between the figures.
-----
Things that often need fixing (put one in the second bracket):
- the frames are different sizes: make frame [N] the same height as the others, feet on the same line
- the legs: in frame [N] the [left/right] foot steps forward and the other is behind
- the cloak or hood changed colour: make it the same deep night-blue as the other frames
- there is ground or a shadow under him: remove it, flat magenta only
- the lantern is small or dark: make the lantern in frame [N] bigger, its glass brightly lit warm yellow, like the others
- a figure touches the next one: move it so there is a clear gap
Then download the fixed picture; gemini-next (or putting it in in/hero_<anim>.png and pixi run import-hero) imports it again.
